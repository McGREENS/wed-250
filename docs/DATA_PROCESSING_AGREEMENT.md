## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

### 1.1 Definitions

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined in Rwanda Law No 058/2021.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, transmission, erasure, or destruction.

**"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable natural person whose personal data is processed.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party appointed by the Processor to process personal data on behalf of the Controller.

**"Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to personal data.

**"Supervisory Authority"** means the National Cyber Security Authority of Rwanda.

**"Services"** means [describe services provided by Processor].

### 1.2 Interpretation

References to:
- **"Rwanda Law No 058/2021"** include any amendments or replacements
- **"Articles"** refer to articles of Rwanda Law No 058/2021 unless otherwise stated
- **"Working days"** exclude weekends and public holidays in Rwanda

## 3. Processor Obligations

### 3.1 Processing Instructions (Article 48)

The Processor shall:
- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller
- Not process personal data for any other purpose
- Immediately inform the Controller if instructions violate Rwanda Law No 058/2021
- Cease processing if instructed by the Controller

**Documented Instructions**: This Agreement and any written instructions provided by the Controller.

### 3.2 Confidentiality (Article 48)

The Processor shall:
- Ensure all personnel authorized to process personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Maintain confidentiality during and after the Agreement
- Limit access to personal data to personnel who need it for the Services
- Implement confidentiality agreements with all personnel

### 3.3 Security Measures (Articles 46-47)

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

**Technical Measures**:
- Encryption of personal data in transit and at rest
- Pseudonymization where appropriate
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security testing and assessment
- Secure backup and recovery procedures
- Network security (firewalls, intrusion detection)

**Organizational Measures**:
- Security policies and procedures
- Staff training on data protection
- Access management and authorization
- Incident response procedures
- Business continuity planning
- Regular security audits

**Security Standards**: [Specify, e.g., "ISO 27001 certified", "SOC 2 Type II compliant"]

Detailed security measures are set out in **Annex B: Security Measures**.

### 3.4 Sub-processing (Article 49)

**General Authorization**: The Controller provides general authorization for the Processor to engage sub-processors, subject to the following conditions:

- Prior written notice to the Controller (at least 30 days)
- Controller's right to object on reasonable grounds (within 14 days)
- Written contract with sub-processor imposing same obligations as this Agreement
- Processor remains fully liable for sub-processor's performance

**Current Sub-processors**: Listed in **Annex C: Sub-processors**.

**New Sub-processors**: The Processor shall notify the Controller before engaging any new sub-processor.

### 3.5 Data Subject Rights (Articles 52-57)

The Processor shall:
- Assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests
- Implement technical measures to facilitate rights exercise
- Respond to Controller's requests for assistance within 5 working days
- Not respond directly to data subjects unless instructed by Controller

**Data Subject Rights**:
- Right of access (Article 52)
- Right to rectification (Article 53)
- Right to erasure (Article 54)
- Right to restriction (Article 55)
- Right to data portability (Article 56)
- Right to object (Article 57)

### 3.6 Data Breach Notification (Articles 43-45)

The Processor shall:
- Notify the Controller without undue delay (maximum 24 hours) upon becoming aware of a data breach
- Provide sufficient information to enable the Controller to meet its notification obligations
- Cooperate with the Controller in investigating and remediating the breach
- Document all data breaches

**Breach Notification Details** (minimum):
- Nature of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
- Categories and approximate number of records affected
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach
- Contact point for further information

### 3.7 Data Protection Impact Assessment (Article 38)

The Processor shall:
- Assist the Controller in conducting DPIAs when required
- Provide information about processing activities and security measures
- Cooperate in consultations with the Supervisory Authority if required

### 3.8 Audits and Inspections

The Processor shall:
- Allow the Controller to conduct audits (with reasonable notice)
- Provide information and access necessary for audits
- Allow independent auditors appointed by the Controller
- Cooperate fully with audit activities

**Audit Rights**:
- Frequency: Annual audits, plus ad-hoc if data breach or suspected non-compliance
- Notice: 30 days' notice (except in case of breach)
- Scope: All processing activities and security measures
- Cost: Controller bears cost of audits (except if non-compliance found)

### 3.9 Records of Processing (Article 17)

The Processor shall maintain records of all processing activities, including:
- Name and contact details of Processor and Controller
- Categories of processing
- Description of security measures
- International transfers (if applicable)

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

### 6.1 Retention Period

The Processor shall retain personal data only for the duration necessary to provide the Services, unless:
- Longer retention is required by law
- Controller instructs otherwise in writing

### 6.2 Data Return or Deletion

Upon termination of the Agreement or upon Controller's request, the Processor shall (at Controller's choice):

**Option 1: Return**
- Return all personal data to the Controller in a structured, commonly used format
- Provide certification of return

**Option 2: Deletion**
- Securely delete all personal data
- Provide certification of deletion

**Timeline**: Within 30 days of termination or request

**Exceptions**: Data may be retained if required by law, with notification to Controller.

### 6.3 Backup Data

Backup copies shall be:
- Securely stored and isolated
- Deleted in accordance with backup retention policy (maximum 90 days)
- Subject to same security measures as active data

## 8. Term and Termination

### 8.1 Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the duration of the Services Agreement.

### 8.2 Termination

This Agreement may be terminated:
- By either party with 30 days' written notice
- Immediately by Controller if Processor breaches material terms
- Immediately by Controller if Processor is subject to insolvency proceedings
- Upon termination of the Services Agreement

### 8.3 Effects of Termination

Upon termination:
- Processor shall cease all processing immediately
- Processor shall return or delete all personal data (Section 6.2)
- Processor shall certify compliance with deletion/return
- Confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely
- Audit rights survive for 2 years
