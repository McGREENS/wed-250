## Purpose of Data Processing

This consent form is required under Rwanda Law No 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy. By providing your consent, you authorize Andiza Co. Limited to process your personal data for the following purposes:

- Account creation and management
- Wedding planning and vendor matching services
- Booking and payment processing
- Communication regarding services and bookings
- Platform improvement and analytics
- Marketing communications (optional)

## Personal Data Categories

We will collect and process the following categories of personal data:

- **Identity Data**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Account Data**: Username, password (encrypted), profile information
- **Booking Data**: Service bookings, venue reservations, payment information
- **Communication Data**: Messages, reviews, feedback
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser type, device information, cookies
- **Usage Data**: Platform activity, preferences, search history

## Legal Basis for Processing

Your consent serves as the legal basis for processing your personal data under Article 6 of Rwanda Law No 058/2021. For certain processing activities, we may rely on:

- Contract performance (Article 7)
- Legal obligations (Article 8)
- Legitimate interests (Article 9)

## Data Recipients

Your personal data may be shared with:

- Wedding vendors and venues (for booking purposes)
- Payment processors (for transaction processing)
- Email service providers (for communications)
- Cloud hosting providers (for data storage)
- Legal authorities (when required by law)

## Data Retention Period

Your personal data will be retained for:

- Active accounts: Duration of account existence plus 2 years
- Booking records: 7 years (for legal and accounting purposes)
- Marketing data: Until consent is withdrawn
- Technical logs: 12 months

## Your Rights

Under Rwanda Law No 058/2021, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data (Article 52)
- Rectify inaccurate data (Article 53)
- Erase your data (Article 54)
- Restrict processing (Article 55)
- Data portability (Article 56)
- Object to processing (Article 57)
- Withdraw consent at any time (Article 10)
- Lodge a complaint with the National Cyber Security Authority
