## 5. What You Should Do

We recommend you take the following steps to protect yourself:

### Immediate Actions (Do This Now)

**1. Change Your Password**
- Log in to your Andiza account at https://andiza.rw
- Go to Settings > Security
- Change your password to a strong, unique password
- Do not reuse passwords from other websites

**2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
- Go to Settings > Security
- Enable two-factor authentication for added security
- You will receive a code via email when logging in

**3. Review Your Account Activity**
- Check your account for any suspicious activity
- Review recent bookings and messages
- Report anything unusual to us immediately

### Additional Precautions

**4. Be Alert for Phishing**
- Be cautious of emails, calls, or messages asking for personal information
- We will never ask for your password via email or phone
- Verify the sender before clicking links or opening attachments
- Report suspicious communications to us

**5. Monitor Your Accounts**
- Check your email account for suspicious activity
- Monitor your bank and credit card statements
- Report any unauthorized transactions immediately

**6. Update Passwords on Other Sites**
- If you used the same password on other websites, change those passwords too
- Use unique passwords for each website
- Consider using a password manager

[If payment information affected:]

**7. Monitor Your Financial Accounts**
- Check your bank and credit card statements regularly
- Report any unauthorized transactions to your bank immediately
- Consider placing a fraud alert on your credit file
- We are offering [credit monitoring service/identity theft protection] at no cost to you
