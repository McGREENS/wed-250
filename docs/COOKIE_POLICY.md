## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They help websites:
- Remember your preferences
- Improve your experience
- Understand how you use the site
- Deliver relevant content

---

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

**Purpose**: Enable core website functionality  
**Consent Required**: No (required for website operation)  
**Duration**: Session or up to 1 year

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| session_token | Maintain user login session | Session |
| csrf_token | Prevent cross-site request forgery | Session |
| cookie_consent | Remember your cookie preferences | 1 year |

**Legal Basis**: Legitimate interest (Article 9, Rwanda Law No 058/2021)

### 3.2 Functional Cookies (Preference Cookies)

**Purpose**: Remember your choices and preferences  
**Consent Required**: Yes  
**Duration**: Up to 1 year

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| language_pref | Remember language selection | 1 year |
| theme_pref | Remember dark/light mode | 1 year |
| location_pref | Remember location filter | 6 months |
| view_mode | Remember list/grid view preference | 6 months |

**Legal Basis**: Consent (Article 6, Rwanda Law No 058/2021)

### 3.3 Analytics Cookies (Performance Cookies)

**Purpose**: Understand how visitors use our website  
**Consent Required**: Yes  
**Duration**: Up to 12 months

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| _ga | Google Analytics - distinguish users | 2 years |
| _gid | Google Analytics - distinguish users | 24 hours |
| _gat | Google Analytics - throttle requests | 1 minute |
| analytics_session | Track session duration | Session |

**Data Collected**:
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Click patterns
- Device and browser information
- Anonymized IP address

**Legal Basis**: Consent (Article 6, Rwanda Law No 058/2021)

### 3.4 Marketing Cookies (Advertising Cookies)

**Purpose**: Deliver relevant advertisements and measure effectiveness  
**Consent Required**: Yes  
**Duration**: Up to 12 months

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| _fbp | Facebook Pixel - track conversions | 3 months |
| ads_pref | Remember ad preferences | 1 year |
| marketing_id | Track marketing campaigns | 6 months |

**Legal Basis**: Consent (Article 6, Rwanda Law No 058/2021)

## 6. How to Manage Cookies

### 6.1 Cookie Consent Banner
When you first visit our website, you can choose:
- **Accept All**: Allow all cookies
- **Essential Only**: Allow only necessary cookies
- **Customize**: Select specific cookie categories

### 6.2 Change Your Preferences
- Click "Cookie Settings" in the website footer
- Update your preferences at any time
- Changes take effect immediately

### 6.3 Browser Settings

**Google Chrome:**
1. Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
2. Choose your preferred option
3. Manage exceptions for specific sites

**Mozilla Firefox:**
1. Settings > Privacy & Security
2. Choose "Custom" under Enhanced Tracking Protection
3. Select cookie preferences

**Safari:**
1. Preferences > Privacy
2. Choose "Block all cookies" or customize
3. Manage website data

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Settings > Cookies and site permissions
2. Manage and delete cookies
3. Block third-party cookies

### 6.4 Mobile Browsers

**iOS Safari:**
Settings > Safari > Block All Cookies

**Android Chrome:**
Settings > Site settings > Cookies

---

## 7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

### 7.1 Essential Cookies Disabled
- Cannot log in to your account
- Cannot make bookings
- Website functionality severely limited
- Security features may not work

### 7.2 Functional Cookies Disabled
- Preferences not remembered
- Must re-enter settings each visit
- Reduced user experience

### 7.3 Analytics Cookies Disabled
- No impact on functionality
- We cannot improve website based on usage data

### 7.4 Marketing Cookies Disabled
- No impact on functionality
- May see less relevant advertisements
- Cannot measure marketing effectiveness

## 8. Cookie Lifespan

### 8.1 Session Cookies
- Deleted when you close your browser
- Used for temporary functionality

### 8.2 Persistent Cookies
- Remain until expiry date or manual deletion

| Category | Typical Duration |
