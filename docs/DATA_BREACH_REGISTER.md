## 1. Purpose and Legal Requirement

### 1.1 Purpose
This register documents all personal data breaches in compliance with Article 45 of Rwanda Law No 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy.

### 1.2 Legal Requirement
The data controller must maintain a register of all data breaches, including:
- Facts relating to the breach
- Effects of the breach
- Remedial action taken

### 1.3 Retention Period
Breach records shall be retained for 10 years from the date of the breach.

## 3. Breach Classification

### 3.1 Severity Levels

**CRITICAL**: Large-scale breach with high risk of severe harm  
**HIGH**: Significant breach with high risk of harm  
**MEDIUM**: Moderate breach with moderate risk  
**LOW**: Minor breach with minimal risk

### 3.2 Breach Types

**C**: Confidentiality breach (unauthorized access/disclosure)  
**I**: Integrity breach (unauthorized alteration)  
**A**: Availability breach (loss of access)
