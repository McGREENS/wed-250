## Right to Withdraw Parental Consent

Under Rwanda Law No 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy, parents or legal guardians have the right to withdraw consent for processing a minor's personal data at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

## Consequences of Withdrawal

Please note the following consequences:

**Complete Withdrawal:**
- The minor's account will be permanently closed
- The minor will no longer be able to access platform services
- All personal data will be erased within 30 days (except where retention is legally required)
- Active bookings or commitments may be affected

**Partial Withdrawal:**
- Selected services may become unavailable
- Platform functionality may be limited
- The minor can continue using other services with restrictions

**Temporary Suspension:**
- The account will be deactivated but not deleted
- Data will be retained for up to 6 months
- The account can be reactivated by submitting a new consent form

**Legal Retention:**
- Some data must be retained for legal obligations
- Data required for ongoing contracts will be retained until contract completion
