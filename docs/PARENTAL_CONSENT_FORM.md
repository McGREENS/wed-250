## Purpose of This Form

Under Rwanda Law No 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy, special protection is required for processing personal data of minors (persons under 18 years of age). This form is required to obtain parental or legal guardian consent before processing a minor's personal data.

## Purpose of Data Processing

The minor's personal data will be processed for the following purposes:

- [ ] Account creation and management
- [ ] Wedding planning services (as a participant or helper)
- [ ] Communication regarding services
- [ ] Platform access and usage

## Personal Data to be Collected

The following categories of personal data will be collected from the minor:

- **Identity Data**: Name, email address, phone number
- **Account Data**: Username, password (encrypted), profile information
- **Communication Data**: Messages, feedback
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser type, device information
- **Usage Data**: Platform activity, preferences

## Data Recipients

The minor's personal data may be shared with:

- Wedding vendors and venues (for service purposes)
- Email service providers (for communications)
- Cloud hosting providers (for data storage)
- Legal authorities (when required by law)

## Data Retention Period

The minor's personal data will be retained for:

- Active accounts: Duration of account existence plus 2 years
- Communication records: 2 years
- Technical logs: 12 months

## Parental Rights and Responsibilities

As a parent or legal guardian, you have the right to:

- Access the minor's personal data at any time
- Request rectification of inaccurate data
- Request erasure of the minor's data
- Withdraw this consent at any time
- Lodge a complaint with the National Cyber Security Authority

You are responsible for:

- Supervising the minor's use of the platform
- Ensuring the minor understands privacy and data protection
- Monitoring communications and activities
- Updating consent as circumstances change

## Minor's Rights Upon Reaching Majority

When the minor reaches 18 years of age:

- They will assume full control of their account
- They can provide their own consent for data processing
- Parental consent will no longer be required
- They will be notified of their rights and responsibilities
