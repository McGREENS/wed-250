## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This Data Retention Policy establishes guidelines for retaining and disposing of personal data in compliance with Rwanda Law No 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy.

### 1.2 Objectives
- Retain data only as long as necessary for specified purposes
- Comply with legal and regulatory retention requirements
- Minimize privacy risks through timely data disposal
- Ensure business continuity and operational efficiency
- Support data subject rights and legal obligations

### 1.3 Scope
This policy applies to:
- All personal data collected and processed by Andiza Co. Limited
- All data storage systems (databases, file systems, backups, archives)
- All employees, contractors, and third-party processors
- All business units and departments

---

## 2. Legal and Regulatory Framework

### 2.1 Primary Legislation
- **Rwanda Law No 058/2021**: Storage limitation principle
- **Tax and Accounting Laws**: 7-year retention for financial records
- **Contract Law**: Retention for limitation periods
- **Employment Law**: Employee record retention

### 2.2 Data Protection Principles
- **Storage Limitation**: Retain data no longer than necessary
- **Purpose Limitation**: Retain only for original or compatible purposes
- **Data Minimization**: Delete unnecessary data promptly
- **Accountability**: Document retention decisions and actions

---

## 3. Retention Principles

### 3.1 Necessity Test
Data is retained only when necessary for:
- Fulfilling the original purpose
- Complying with legal obligations
- Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims
- Archiving in the public interest (rare)

### 3.2 Retention Period Determination
Based on:
- **Legal requirements**: Statutory retention periods
- **Business needs**: Operational and contractual requirements
- **Risk assessment**: Litigation and dispute risks
- **Data subject rights**: Balancing retention with privacy rights

### 3.3 Active vs. Archived Data
- **Active data**: Readily accessible for operational use
- **Archived data**: Stored securely with restricted access
- **Backup data**: Follows same retention as source data

## 5. Retention Period Extensions

### 5.1 Legal Hold
Retention periods may be extended when:
- Litigation is pending or reasonably anticipated
- Regulatory investigation is ongoing
- Legal claims are being pursued or defended
- Court order or subpoena requires retention

**Process**:
1. Legal team issues legal hold notice
2. IT implements technical hold on affected data
3. Data is preserved until hold is lifted
4. Normal retention resumes after hold release

### 5.2 Business Need Extension
Retention may be extended for legitimate business needs:
- Ongoing contract or relationship
- Unresolved disputes or complaints
- Regulatory audit or examination
- Business continuity requirements

**Approval**: DPO and Legal Team

## 6. Data Disposal Procedures

### 6.1 Secure Deletion Methods

**Digital Data**:
- **Standard deletion**: Overwrite with random data (3 passes minimum)
- **Cryptographic erasure**: Delete encryption keys
- **Physical destruction**: Destroy storage media (hard drives, SSDs)
- **Degaussing**: Magnetic erasure for magnetic media

**Physical Records**:
- **Shredding**: Cross-cut shredding (DIN P-4 or higher)
- **Pulping**: Industrial pulping for large volumes
- **Incineration**: Secure incineration with certificate

### 6.2 Disposal Verification
- Certificate of destruction for physical media
- Deletion logs for digital data
- Audit trail of disposal actions
- Periodic verification audits

### 6.3 Third-Party Disposal
When using third-party disposal services:
- Vendor security assessment
- Confidentiality agreements
- Certificate of destruction required
- Audit rights in contract

## 8. Exceptions and Special Cases

### 8.1 Anonymized Data
Anonymized data (irreversibly de-identified) is not subject to retention limits and may be retained indefinitely for:
- Statistical analysis
- Research purposes
- Business intelligence

**Requirement**: Anonymization must be irreversible and meet legal standards.

### 8.2 Aggregated Data
Aggregated data (no individual identification possible) may be retained indefinitely for:
- Trend analysis
- Reporting
- Platform improvement

### 8.3 Public Information
Publicly available information (e.g., published reviews) may be retained longer but:
- Data subjects retain right to erasure
- Legitimate interest must be balanced
- Regular review required

---

## 9. Data Subject Rights and Retention

### 9.1 Right to Erasure
Data subjects can request deletion before retention period expires if:
- Data no longer necessary for original purpose
- Consent is withdrawn
- Data was unlawfully processed
- Legal obligation requires erasure

**Exceptions** (retention continues):
- Legal obligation to retain
- Legal claims defense
- Public interest archiving

### 9.2 Right to Restriction
Data subjects can request restriction instead of deletion:
- Data is marked as restricted
- Processing is limited
- Data is retained but not actively used
