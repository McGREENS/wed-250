# Andiza Wedding Platform - Pricing Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document outlines a freemium pricing model for Andiza's wedding planning platform, designed to maximize user acquisition while generating sustainable revenue. The strategy balances free access to essential features with premium upgrades that deliver real value.

---

## Market Positioning

**Target Market**: Rwanda wedding market  
**Platform Type**: Two-sided marketplace (Couples + Vendors)  
**Revenue Model**: Freemium with tiered subscriptions  
**Currency**: RWF (Rwandan Franc)

---

## Upgrade/Downgrade Policy

### Upgrades:
- Instant access to new features
- Prorated billing for remaining period
- All data preserved

### Downgrades:
- Take effect at end of billing period
- Data preserved but features locked
- 30-day grace period to re-upgrade without data loss

### Cancellation:
- Can cancel anytime
- Access until end of paid period
- Data preserved for 90 days
- Export available before deletion

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Concerns:

**1. Free users leave when paid:**
- Mitigation: Generous free tier, gradual rollout, grandfather period

**2. Price too high for market:**
- Mitigation: Market research, competitor analysis, flexible pricing

**3. Low conversion rates:**
- Mitigation: In-app education, trial periods, success stories

**4. Vendor reluctance:**
- Mitigation: ROI calculator, case studies, money-back guarantee

**5. Payment infrastructure issues:**
- Mitigation: Multiple payment options, local payment methods, support

---

## Conclusion

This freemium strategy balances **accessibility** (free tier), **value** (Essential/Professional), and **premium experience** (Premium/Business). The pricing is designed to:

1. **Acquire users** through generous free plans
2. **Convert to paid** when real value is demonstrated
3. **Retain customers** through continuous feature development
4. **Maximize revenue** through tiered value delivery

The platform's unique features (wedding helpers, integrated payments, task management, invoicing) provide clear upgrade motivations while maintaining a usable free tier that serves as a powerful acquisition tool.
