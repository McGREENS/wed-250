- Wedding data: Wedding date, location, budget, guest count

## 9. Your Rights

Under Rwanda Law No 058/2021, you have the following rights:

### 9.1 Right of Access (Article 52)
Request access to your personal data and obtain a copy.

### 9.2 Right to Rectification (Article 53)
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

### 9.3 Right to Erasure (Article 54)
Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

### 9.4 Right to Restriction (Article 55)
Request limitation of processing in certain circumstances.

### 9.5 Right to Data Portability (Article 56)
Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

### 9.6 Right to Object (Article 57)
Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for marketing.

### 9.7 Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 10)
Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

### 9.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint
File a complaint with the National Cyber Security Authority.

**How to Exercise Your Rights:**
- Email: privacy@andiza.co.rw or dpo@andiza.co.rw
- Online forms: https://andiza.co.rw/privacy
- Response time: Within 30 days
